## The inspiration

A few that shaped this one:

- [alexwlchan.net](https://alexwlchan.net) - really like the old + modern custom styled website
- [areyouelectronic.com](https://www.areyouelectronic.com) and [cassidoo.co](https://cassidoo.co) - just really vibrant sites
- [lazybea.rs](https://lazybea.rs) and [helgesver.re](https://helgesver.re) - for taking the bear blog template

I also included the [now page](https://nownownow.com), the[slash pages](https://slashpages.net) convention, and [uses.tech](https://uses.tech).

## Tech Stack
- **[Astro](https://astro.build)** 
- **Tailwind v4** 
- **MDX** 
- **Expressive Code**
- **Vercel** for hosting
- Clicky button sound

Underneath it's basically markdown files and a bit of CSS.
## the look
custom themes, clicky button sounds, minimalist
